# CueX Brand Master

CueX (**你的快思考外挂 · 在深度思考之外，接住当下**) uses a **Warm Paper Daylight (`#F8F7F3`)** editorial aesthetic paired with the two-stroke **Amber × Violet crossed `X`** symbol. The first stroke (Amber `#E87A24` / `#FF7A2F`) denotes live human delivery and fast timing signals; the second stroke (Violet `#7C3AED` / `#8E8CFF`) denotes background Universal Cognitive Runtime comprehension and grounded evidence. Their minimal intersection represents the `L0–L3` intervention decision across arbitrary real-world interaction scenarios (`X`).

## Vector & Mark Assets (`assets/brand/`)

- `cuex-symbol.svg`: primary two-stroke `X` mark for icons, favicons, and compact badges.
- `cuex-symbol-dimensional.svg` (with `x-dimensional.png` & `x-model.json`): dimensional two-ribbon `X` mark used in web navigation, hero lockups, and presentation covers.
- `cuex-symbol-mono.svg`: single-ink `X` for monochrome print or hairline marks.
- `cuex-wordmark.svg`: outlined `Instrument Serif` “Cue” + `X` for light Warm Paper surfaces.
- `cuex-wordmark-light.svg`: outlined wordmark for dark contrast surfaces when required.

Outlined wordmarks require no installed system fonts. Do not add unrelated icon geometry or neon drop-shadows.

## Palette

- **Warm Paper Ground**: `#F8F7F3` (primary page, web stage, slide deck, and print background; avoid dark-neon or muted-slate themes)
- **Soft Cream Surface**: `#FFFFFF` / `#F2EFE7` (cards, tables, and structured cue panels)
- **Charcoal Ink / Muted Ink**: `#111318` / `#555863` (high-contrast typography)
- **Paper Hairline**: `#D9D6CE` (1 px borders and dividers)
- **Cue Amber**: `#E87A24` / `#FF7A2F` (`L1` edge cues, speech flow, key pacing highlights)
- **Runtime Violet**: `#7C3AED` / `#8E8CFF` (Universal Runtime comprehension, `System 2` & grounded `Evidence`)
- **Restrained Semantic Signals**: Sage Pass `#246A4D` / Alert Rust `#C94A2A` (used sparingly for verification pass, withdrawal, or `L3` escalation)

## Typography (`assets/fonts/`)

- **Brand & English Display**: `Instrument Serif` (`InstrumentSerif-Regular.ttf`, `InstrumentSerif-Italic.ttf`)
- **Chinese Display Headlines**: `Noto Serif SC` (`NotoSerifSC-VF.ttf`)
- **Product UI & Body Copy**: `Noto Sans SC` (`NotoSansSC-VF.ttf`) & `Inter` (`Inter-VF.ttf`)
- **Telemetry, Protocol & Equations**: `JetBrains Mono` (`JetBrainsMono-VF.ttf`, with `tabular-nums`)
